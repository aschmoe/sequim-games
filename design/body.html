Sequim Games

A California Games style collection for two brothers-in-law. Four short events, one for each of their interests, played in the phone's browser. Every screen below is drawn at the game's real resolution: 320 by 180 pixels, scaled up.

SEBASTIAN · ELECTRIC SKATEBOARD

Volt Run

PLAYABLE NOW

Sebastian rides home through three stretches: downtown, the waterfront bike path under the Olympics, and the forest trails. The catch is the battery: throttle drains it and only lightning bolts refill it, so coast where you can. Each stretch has a patch of rough ground (cobblestones, gravel, rocks) with a 16 mph limit, and hitting it too fast shakes you off the board. Run dry and he kick-pushes the rest of the way. Sophia is waiting at the finish line with a sign.

CONTROLS
Hold the right side to go, hold the left side to brake, swipe up to hop.
HAZARDS
Cones, potholes and speed bumps downtown. Hans (a big black dog) and Lexie (a golden retriever) dart across the bike path. Roots and rocks on the trails. Misha stands on the sidewalk rehearsing Hamlet: hop over him, or slow down and he steps aside.
PICKUPS
Lightning bolts float in the air, so you hop to grab them (+8% each). Coffee gives a short speed burst with no battery drain.
SCORE
Finish time, battery left, bolts grabbed, minus wipeouts. A careful run lands around 10,000. A dead-battery run lands around 4,500.
THE BIT
Speed wobble. Hold the throttle down a hill and the screen starts to shake. Let off the gas or eat pavement. Coasting is always safe.
DECIDEDGeneric board, all three settings, helmet on.
SEBASTIAN · MOOG SYNTH

Patch Panic

PLAYABLE NOW

A Moog-style modular rack and a handful of patch cables, with Sebastian at the controls in the corner. Three rounds: Bass Line (100 bpm, 3 cables, with guide lines), Acid (110 bpm, 4 cables) and Filter Sweep (120 bpm, 5 cables). Each opens with a patch card: drag cables between the jacks before the timer runs out, and the faster you finish, the bigger the bonus. After the first round the guide lines go away, but each output that still needs a cable blinks in its card color, and if you hold a cable for a second its destination lights up. Then the song starts: pulses travel down your cables and you tap each jack as a pulse lands. Every hit plays a lead note over a synthesized backing track, and a miss plays a sour one.

CONTROLS
Drag to plug a cable. Tap a jack on the beat (or D F J K L on a keyboard). During the sweep, a slider panel appears: keep the dot in the moving green band by sliding your finger up and down.
CHAOS
In rounds 2 and 3 a cable pops out mid-song and dangles. Drag the plug back in before the groove meter drains.
SEBASTIAN
His portrait reacts to how you're playing: nodding on the beat when the groove is on, grimacing and sweating after a miss, and sunglasses on at a 16-note combo.
SCORE
Perfect and good hits with a combo multiplier, the patch bonus, the sweep, and the groove meter at the end. A wrong jack costs 100.
DECIDEDMoog gear, electronic music, and an original backing track written for the game.
MISHA · AVANT-GARDE THEATRE

Opening Night

PLAYABLE NOW

THE LAVENDER KING, an avant-garde tragedy in three acts of three beats. Act I: a stranger arrives in Sequim, meets a bearded farmer and is offered sweet white wine. Act II: the wine runs out, Hans and Lexie look very guilty, and he claims the throne. Act III: the fog rolls in, the dogs come back with a bottle, and he gives his final words. About two and a half minutes.

THE VIBE
Two wheels set each act's mood (menacing, joyful, bored, romantic, panicked, regal) and style (operatic, whispered, robotic, Shakespearean, interpretive).
THE SCRIPT
A prompt book on the left runs each beat as CUE, MOVE, ACTION, LINE, BOW on a musical count. The step in progress opens up with its options; once you choose, it rolls up into a line showing your choice and its pips, so the whole performance so far stays readable.
MOVE
A real stage direction (DOWNSTAGE LEFT is the actor's left, your right). Walk the 2D stage with a hot/cold meter; the mark is revealed with how many feet off you were.
ACTION
Three story actions, one per style. When he's offered wine: raise the glass to the rafters, sip it like a secret, drink in 3 precise motions, toast the heavens, or become the wine. Scored against the act's style, and Misha strikes the matching pose.
LINE
Three lines, the same story moment in different moods. Scored against the act's mood.
TIMING
Generous windows, but the play moves on the count after you choose, with a bonus for picking quickly.
BOW
Roses float down slowly onto pink target rings; go catch them. Tomatoes are fast and aimed where you're heading.
REDESIGNED TWICENow three beats per act, story actions instead of generic poses, a rolling prompt book, longer windows that end early when you choose, and fairer roses with nastier tomatoes.
MISHA · DUNGEONS & DRAGONS

Roll for Initiative

PLAYABLE NOW

A four-room dungeon crawl starring Misha. Pick his class, then fight through the Goblin Den, More Goblins, the Orc's Cellar, and the Volt Throne, where Sebastian the Terrible, Lord of the Volts, waits on his electric board. It runs in real time: everyone has a charge gauge, Misha acts the moment his fills, and each enemy strikes the moment its own fills. About two minutes.

CLASSES
Fighter (34 HP, Cleave: one timed swing at every enemy). Wizard (22 HP, two Fireballs: tap as fast as you can for two seconds to power one up). Bard (26 HP, Vicious Mockery: pick the insult that hits the enemy's listed weakness for big damage and a stun).
ATTACK
Time slows while you aim. A marker sweeps across a bar; stop it near the center for a high d20. Dead center is a natural 20 and a critical hit; the very edge is a natural 1 and Misha trips over his cape.
BLOCK
When an enemy strikes, a ring closes on Misha. One tap per strike: perfect blocks take nothing, good ones take half, early or late takes it all. Rings differ by attack, and the orc's smash stalls halfway to catch early tappers.
DECISIONS
Every enemy shows its next move and its gauge, so you can race to kill the orc before its 14-damage smash lands. Whenever you're ready: attack, your special, a Sweet White Wine (heals 12), or Guard (halves damage for 6 seconds). Between rooms, pick a reward: more wine, more max HP, more damage or another special use.
THE BOSS
Sebastian zaps you with lightning bolts. Every third attack his battery runs low and he plugs into the wall for a few seconds: his guard drops and your damage doubles, so hit him fast. Below half health he zaps twice.
DECIDEDAlways Misha, three classes, Sebastian as the boss, sweet white wine for potions.
Look, sound, and build

1987 on the screen, 2026 under the hood

Look & sound

  • Art: everything drawn in pixels at 320 by 180, scaled up with no smoothing, landscape only.
  • Type: Press Start 2P for headlines and scores, Silkscreen for small text.
  • Music: one short chiptune loop per event, plus a title theme.
  • Sound effects: generated in code (ZzFX), so no audio files to manage.
  • Patch Panic uses real Web Audio oscillators and filters, so patching actually changes the sound.

Build & ship

  • Engine: plain canvas and JavaScript, no framework. All the art is drawn in code, so a game engine wouldn't add much.
  • Adding events: a shared shell runs the title, menu, pause, results, name entry, and scores. Each event is one self-contained file that registers itself, so a fifth game is one new file.
  • Hosting: one Docker setup on your DigitalOcean server. Caddy serves the game with automatic HTTPS.
  • Scoreboard: a tiny Node and SQLite service next to it. Top 10 per event, no accounts. The prototype keeps scores on your phone until then.
  • Links: /sebastian and /misha list that brother's events first.
  • Testing: a work-in-progress link you can open on your phone throughout, then a final pass in Chrome on Android.
